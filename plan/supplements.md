# Supplements

Short list on purpose. These three have real evidence behind them; nearly
everything else is noise on top of food and sleep.

## Creatine monohydrate — start now

- **5 g per day, every day.** That's it.
- **Any time of day.** Timing doesn't matter. Take it whenever you'll remember —
  same time as some other daily habit is easiest.
- **Rest days too.** You're topping up a store, not fuelling a session.
- **Monohydrate only.** It's the cheapest form and the one all the research used.
  HCl, "Kre-Alkalyn", buffered, liquid — all more expensive, none better. Creapure
  is a quality label; fine, not required.
- **No loading phase needed.** 20 g/day for a week saturates you in ~7 days instead
  of ~28, and gives more people stomach upset. On a 12-week block, waiting 3 extra
  weeks costs nothing.
- Mix into anything. Warm water dissolves it better; it doesn't need to fully
  dissolve to work.

### What to expect

- **+1–2 kg on the scale in weeks 1–3.** This is water pulled *into* the muscle.
  It is not fat and it is not bloat. Do not react to it, and do not use those
  weeks to judge whether the bulk is working (nutrition.md says the same).
- A few % more reps on sets in the 6–12 range, showing up within 2–4 weeks.
- Muscles look fuller. That's the same water.
- It does **not** damage kidneys in healthy people — that's a persistent myth from
  a misread of creatinine blood markers, which creatine legitimately raises without
  anything being wrong. If you have existing kidney disease, ask a doctor first.
- If you get GI discomfort: take it with food, or split into 2 × 2.5 g.

## Vitamin D3 — 2000–4000 IU/day

Worth it through autumn and winter at most latitudes. Affects sleep, mood and
testosterone at the margin. Cheap.

## Whey protein — optional, it's food not a supplement

Only useful if you're missing the 150 g protein target. One scoop ≈ 25 g and takes
30 seconds, which beats a fourth chicken breast you won't cook. If you hit protein
with food, skip it.

## Not worth buying

BCAAs (pointless if protein is adequate), test boosters, mass gainers (sugar at a
markup — just eat), fat burners, pre-workout for anything other than the caffeine.

If you want the caffeine: 100–200 mg about 45 min pre-session, and not within
8 hours of bed. Sleep is worth more than any pre-workout.
