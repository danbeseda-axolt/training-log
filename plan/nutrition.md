# Nutrition — Lean Bulk

Starting bodyweight: **71.5 kg** (2026-09-17)

## Targets

| | Grams | kcal |
|---|---|---|
| Protein | **150 g** | 600 |
| Fat | **75 g** | 675 |
| Carbs | **370 g** | 1480 |
| **Total** | | **~2750 kcal** |

Estimated maintenance is ~2500 kcal, so this is roughly **+250 kcal/day**.

> Caveat: I don't have your height or age, so 2500 is an estimate from bodyweight
> and a moderately-active lifestyle. **The scale is the real answer, not the
> calculator.** See "Adjusting" below — that rule corrects any error in the start point.

## Rate of gain

Target: **+0.2 kg per week** (~0.25% of bodyweight).

- 12 weeks → about **+2.4 kg**, most of it muscle if training and sleep hold up.
- Faster than ~0.35 kg/wk is mostly fat. Slower than ~0.1 kg/wk isn't a bulk.

## Adjusting (do this every Sunday)

1. Weigh in every morning, after the toilet, before food. Write it down.
2. Take the **7-day average**, not any single day.
3. Compare to last week's average:

| This week vs last | Do |
|---|---|
| Gained < 0.1 kg | **+150 kcal/day** (add ~40 g carbs) |
| Gained 0.1–0.35 kg | Hold. This is the target. |
| Gained > 0.35 kg | **−150 kcal/day** |

**Ignore weeks 1–2 of creatine** — see supplements.md, you'll gain 1–2 kg of water
and it will look like the bulk is out of control. It isn't. Start applying this
rule from week 3.

## Practical notes

- **Protein:** 150 g is the number that matters most. Roughly 35–40 g at each of
  4 meals gets you there without thinking about it.
- **Carbs are the point.** The reason 75 kg bench feels like 85 kg right now is
  low muscle glycogen. 370 g/day fixes that within about a week. Put the biggest
  carb meal 2–3 hours before training.
- **Don't eat clean, eat enough.** The failure mode of a lean bulk is under-eating
  because a cut mindset is still running. If you're hitting protein and hitting
  the scale target, the rest is flexible.
- Don't weigh food forever. Two weeks of weighing to calibrate your eye, then
  estimate and let the Sunday scale rule catch the drift.

## What "enough" feels like

By week 2–3 you should notice: warm-ups feel light again, you get the last rep
instead of losing it, you're not cold all the time, sleep is better, and you
actually want to be in the gym. If none of that shows up by week 3, you're not
eating as much as you think you are.
