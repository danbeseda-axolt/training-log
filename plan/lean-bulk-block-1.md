# Lean Bulk — Block 1

**Deload:** 2026-09-17 → 2026-09-20
**Block:** 2026-09-21 → 2026-12-13 (12 weeks)
**Split:** 4 days — Upper / Lower / Upper / Lower

You said you have 6 days available but want to keep 4 training days. Good — 4 is
the right number coming off a cut. The two spare days are for walking, not lifting.
See "The other two days" at the bottom.

---

## Step 0: Deload week (this week, 4 days)

Non-negotiable. You've been under-eating and under-recovering and your lifts are
going backwards. You don't build on that — you reset first.

**For every session this week:**
- Same exercises, **50–60% of your usual working weight**
- **2 sets** of 5–6 reps each
- Every set stops **4+ reps short of failure**. It should feel embarrassingly easy.
- In and out in 35 minutes

Bench = 40–45 kg. Dips = bodyweight only, 6 reps.

Meanwhile: **start eating at the new numbers immediately** (nutrition.md) and
**start creatine today** (supplements.md). Food and creatine don't wait for the
deload to finish.

The point of this week is that you show up to week 1 wanting to train.

---

## Resetting your loads — read this before week 1

You're benching 75 kg and it feels like 85. Your instinct will be to start the
block at 75 and fight for 80. **Don't.** That's the same grinding that stopped
being fun.

Start week 1 at a weight you could get **11–12 reps** with, and stop at 8. For you
that's likely **60–65 kg on bench** and **bodyweight dips**. It will feel too light.
That's correct — that's the runway.

The arithmetic: at +2.5 kg per week on bench you pass 75 kg in week 5 and finish
the block near 90 kg. You do not get there by starting at 75.

**Reps left in reserve (RIR)** — how many more reps you could have done:
- Weeks 1–2: **3–4 RIR** (easy, building the habit back)
- Weeks 3–5: **2 RIR**
- Weeks 7–10: **1–2 RIR**
- Week 11: **0–1 RIR** on the last set of the main lift only
- Never grind a rep that stalls for more than a second.

---

## Progression

For every lift: hit the **top of the rep range on all sets**, then add weight next
session and start again at the bottom of the range.

- Upper body: **+2.5 kg** (or +2 reps if you can't add that little)
- Lower body: **+5 kg**
- Dips/pull-ups: bodyweight until 3×10 clean, then **+2.5 kg** at a time

If you miss the rep target two sessions in a row on the same lift, repeat the
weight a third time rather than dropping it. If it's still stuck, you're either
under-slept or under-fed — check both before changing the program.

Since you have the time but not the recovery to spend it lifting:

- **No** extra hard sessions, no "just arms". On a 4-day plan those days are

## The three rules for the whole block

1. **Eat the calories, especially on rest days.** Under-eating on off days is the
   single most common way a lean bulk quietly becomes maintenance.
2. **Sleep 7–8 hours.** Nothing here works without it. It outranks every
   supplement in supplements.md.
3. **Leave reps in the tank.** You're coming out of a cut that stopped being fun.
   Training to failure every session is how you get back to feeling beaten up,
   and it doesn't build more muscle than stopping 1–2 short.
